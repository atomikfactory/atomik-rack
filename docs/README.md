## Launcher

The Launcher is a second way to make music next to the arrangement: a grid of looping clips that you start and stop
live, and that can be written into the arrangement. View > Launcher (F10) shows it in the lower pane in place of the
arrangement; the same command hides it again.

- **The grid.** Each note track is a column, in the order and the colours of the arrangement's track list; each row is
  a scene. A slot holds one note clip: its name, a small picture of its notes and a play triangle. The scene column on
  the right has a launch button and the scene's name (double click to rename it); the row under the grid has a stop
  square for each track and STOP ALL. A slot that plays has a bar along its bottom edge that follows the loop, and a
  slot that waits for its moment is outlined and blinks. Slots belong to note tracks; audio tracks do not have any yet.
- **Making clips.** Double click an empty slot to make a one-bar note clip and open it in the piano roll; double click
  a clip to open it. Drag a slot to another to move it, with Ctrl (Cmd on macOS) held to copy it. The right-click list
  of a slot renames it, colours it, sets its loop length (1 to 32 bars), sets its launch quantize and duplicates or
  deletes it; the list of a scene inserts, duplicates and deletes scenes. Edit > Copy, Cut, Paste, Duplicate and Delete
  work on the slot under the cursor. View > Launcher > Clip to Launcher puts the selected arrangement clip into the
  slot of its track at the cursor row, and Launcher Clip to Arrangement puts the clip under the cursor at the
  playhead.
- **Launching.** Click a slot's triangle, or move the cursor with the arrows and press Enter; Shift+Enter, or the
  triangle in the scene column, launches the whole scene. A launch waits for the next boundary of the launch quantize
  (the button on the right of the top bar cycles it; View > Launcher > Launch Quantize lists it; None, 1/16, 1/8, 1/4,
  1/2, 1 bar, 2 bars or 4 bars, 1 bar by default; a slot can have its own) and the boundaries are exact through tempo
  changes and meter changes. The clip then plays from its own start and loops. If the transport is stopped, launching
  starts it from where it stands; a position on a bar line launches at once.
- **Launching over the arrangement.** While a track plays a slot, its arrangement clips are silent. The track plays its
  arrangement again when its slot stops (the stop square under the track, or Stop All Clips: both wait for the launch
  quantize) or at once with BACK TO ARRANGEMENT, which is lit while any track plays a slot. With Scene Stops Empty
  Tracks on (the default) a scene launch also stops the tracks that play a slot and have none in that row. Notes that
  are on when a slot stops or another starts are released.
- **Record Launches.** Switch RECORD LAUNCHES on (or View > Launcher > Record Launches), then press Record. Everything
  you launch from then on is written into the arrangement when you stop: each launch becomes a clip of the slot's notes
  at the song position where it really started, as long as it played, looped when it ran longer than the slot. The
  whole pass is one undo step. No track needs to be armed; with armed tracks the notes you play are recorded as usual
  next to it. Press BACK TO ARRANGEMENT afterwards to hear the recording.
- **Not done yet.** Audio clips in slots, dragging clips between the arrangement, the browser and the launcher, launching
  from a MIDI controller, follow actions, and scene tempos that change the song tempo. The notes of a launched slot go
  to the track's instrument like any other notes, so a MIDI keyboard still plays it.

## Privacy

Atomik Rack sends nothing about the user or the computer. Its only network request is the optional check for a newer
version (Help > Check for Updates, and once a day when the user said Yes to the question at the first start): one
HTTPS request to the project's page for the list of releases, carrying only the program name and version as the
User-Agent. Nothing is downloaded or installed, and until the question is answered nothing is sent. How it works is in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#the-update-check); the choice is in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md#s-the-update-check).
